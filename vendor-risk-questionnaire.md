# Vendor Security & Privacy Risk Questionnaire

**Instructions to vendor:** Please answer all questions completely. Where a formal document
(certification, policy, report) supports your answer, attach it as evidence. Incomplete
responses may extend the assessment timeline.

## Section A: Company & Certification Overview
1. Company name, headquarters location, and years in operation
2. Do you hold any of the following current, valid certifications? (attach evidence)
   - ISO/IEC 27001
   - SOC 2 Type II
   - PCI DSS (if applicable to services provided)
   - Other (specify)
3. Have you had a security incident or data breach in the past 24 months? If yes, describe
   scope, impact, and remediation.

## Section B: Data Handling
4. What categories of ExampleOrg data will you access, store, or process?
5. Where is data stored (data center location/cloud region)? Will any data be transferred
   or processed outside India?
6. Is data encrypted at rest? At what standard (e.g., AES-256)?
7. Is data encrypted in transit? (TLS version)
8. What is your data retention policy, and how is data deleted at end of relationship or
   upon request?
9. Do you use subprocessors? If yes, list them and describe your due diligence process for
   subprocessors.

## Section C: Access Control & Authentication
10. Is multi-factor authentication enforced for all employee access to systems holding our data?
11. Describe your access control model (RBAC, least privilege, etc.)
12. How often is access reviewed/recertified?
13. Describe your employee background check / screening process for personnel with data access.

## Section D: Application & Infrastructure Security
14. Do you conduct regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing? At what frequency?
    Attach most recent executive summary/attestation if available.
15. Describe your patch management process and typical patch SLA for critical vulnerabilities.
16. Do you have a documented secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC)?
17. Is production data used in non-production/test environments? If yes, is it masked/anonymized?

## Section E: Incident Response & Business Continuity
18. Do you have a documented incident response plan? When was it last tested?
19. What is your committed breach notification timeline to customers (in hours)?
20. Do you have a business continuity / disaster recovery plan? What are your RTO/RPO commitments?

## Section F: Compliance & Legal
21. Are you willing to sign ExampleOrg's Data Processing Agreement (DPA) as-is, or do you
    require modifications?
22. Do you have a designated Data Protection Officer or equivalent privacy contact?
23. Are you subject to, or compliant with, the Indian DPDP Act 2023 for any India-based processing?

## Section G: Right to Audit
24. Do you accept a right-to-audit clause allowing ExampleOrg (or an independent auditor on
    ExampleOrg's behalf) to review your security controls, with reasonable notice?

**Vendor acknowledgment:** By submitting this questionnaire, the vendor confirms responses
are accurate to the best of their knowledge as of the submission date.
