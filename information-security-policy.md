## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes ExampleOrg Pvt Ltd's commitment to protecting the
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets, and sets the framework
within which all subordinate ISMS policies operate.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all information assets owned, processed, or transmitted by ExampleOrg,
including customer PII, KYC data, transaction data, and internal corporate information, as
defined in the ISMS Scope Statement.

## 3. Policy Statements
1. ExampleOrg shall maintain an Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned to
   ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
2. Information security risk shall be formally assessed and treated per the documented
   Risk Assessment Methodology, at minimum quarterly.
3. All employees and contractors shall complete information security awareness training
   within 30 days of onboarding and annually thereafter.
4. Access to information assets shall be granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis
   and formally reviewed quarterly.
5. All information security incidents shall be reported and handled per the Incident
   Response Policy.
6. Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Violations may result in disciplinary action
   up to and including termination of employment or contract.
7. This policy and all subordinate policies shall be reviewed at least annually, or upon
   material change to the business, threat landscape, or regulatory environment.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO | Ultimate accountability for the ISMS; approves policy and resourcing |
| CISO | Owns the ISMS; chairs quarterly risk review; approves risk treatment decisions |
| Head of GRC | Maintains risk register, SoA, and compliance tracking; runs internal audits |
| Department Heads | Ensure teams comply with policy; report incidents; support access reviews |
| All employees | Comply with policy; complete training; report suspected incidents |

## 5. Enforcement
Non-compliance is addressed via the disciplinary process defined in HR policy. Contractors
and vendors found non-compliant are subject to contractual remedies up to termination of
agreement.
